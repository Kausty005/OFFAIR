INSPECTION FINDINGS
---------------------
Finding 1: VIBRATION - ELEVATED
- Drive end bearing (DE): 4.2 mm/s RMS (ALERT: threshold is 4.5 mm/s, CRITICAL limit)
- Non-drive end bearing (NDE): 3.6 mm/s RMS (ALERT zone)
- Baseline reference: 1.8 mm/s (from commissioning records)
- Dominant frequency: 1x running speed (indicates imbalance or misalignment)
- Conclusion: Vibration elevated at both bearings; NDE shows trend toward critical

Finding 2: BEARING TEMPERATURE - ALERT
- Drive end bearing temperature: 78°C (Alert threshold: 70°C, Critical: 85°C)
- Non-drive end bearing temperature: 72°C (Alert threshold: 70°C)
- Ambient temperature at pump skid: 32°C
- Delta-T (measured minus ambient): DE 46°C, NDE 40°C (limit is 50°C)
- Conclusion: Bearings running warm; possible lubrication issue or incipient failure

Finding 3: MECHANICAL SEAL - SATISFACTORY
- Seal condition: No visible leakage from gland area
- Seal flush piping: Clear, flow observed in sight glass
- Leakage observed: 0 drops per minute (within acceptable limit of <1 drop/minute)
- Seal face temperature (IR thermometer): 42°C (normal range)
- Conclusion: Mechanical seal in satisfactory condition

Finding 4: MOTOR CURRENT - SLIGHTLY ELEVATED
- Measured motor current: 134 A (rated: 128 A, 10% limit = 140.8 A)
- Power factor: 0.86 (normal range 0.80-0.92)
- Motor body temperature: 58°C (normal)
- Conclusion: Motor current slightly elevated, within limit but warrants monitoring

Finding 5: COUPLING AND ALIGNMENT
- Coupling guard: In place and secure
- Coupling condition: No visible rubber element deterioration
- Visual alignment check: Slight angular offset observed at coupling (needs laser check)
- Conclusion: Recommend precision laser alignment during next scheduled stop

Finding 6: SUCTION AND DISCHARGE CONDITIONS
- Suction pressure: 2.1 bar (design: 2.0-2.5 bar) — NORMAL
- Discharge pressure: 9.8 bar (design: 9.5-10.5 bar) — NORMAL
- Flow rate: 115 m3/hr (design: 120 m3/hr, deviation: 4.2%) — WITHIN TOLERANCE
- Differential pressure: 7.7 bar — NORMAL

RISK ASSESSMENT
----------------
Overall Risk Level: HIGH
Justification: Two parameters (DE vibration and bearing temperatures) are in Alert zone with trends toward Critical. If DE vibration reaches 4.5 mm/s, pump must be stopped immediately. Historical trend analysis shows vibration has increased from 1.8 mm/s (baseline) to current 4.2 mm/s over 10 months, indicating progressive bearing deterioration.

RECOMMENDATIONS
-----------------
Priority 1 (Immediate — within 24 hours):
- Increase monitoring frequency to 4-hourly vibration checks
- Assign dedicated operator for P-104 monitoring on each shift
- Prepare P-104's standby pump (P-105) for immediate startup

Priority 2 (Within 7 days — Scheduled Stop):
- Perform precision laser alignment on pump-motor set
- Replace both drive end and non-drive end bearings
- Inspect impeller for erosion or fouling
- Check and adjust impeller running clearances
- Verify bearing lubrication type and quantity

Priority 3 (Annual Overhaul due in 2 months):
- Full pump disassembly and inspection
- Replace mechanical seal as preventative measure
- Inspect wear rings and replace if clearances exceed tolerance
- Motor winding test and insulation resistance test
- Update alignment records in CMMS
