CENTRIFUGAL PUMP MAINTENANCE STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
==========================================================
Document No: SOP-MECH-042 (FICTIONAL DEMO)
Title: Centrifugal Pump Maintenance and Inspection Procedure
Revision: 4 | Date: 2024-01-15 | Approved By: Chief Engineer

1. PURPOSE
----------
This procedure provides guidelines for inspection, maintenance, and overhaul of centrifugal pumps in process plants. This is a fictional demonstration document for SIH26117 OffAir AI Workbench.

2. SCOPE
--------
Applicable to all centrifugal pumps (P-100 to P-200 series) in Process Unit A and B.
Equipment types: Single-stage and multi-stage centrifugal pumps up to 500 kW.

3. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------
3.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
- Safety helmet, safety glasses, hearing protection
- Chemical-resistant gloves and safety boots
- Flame-retardant coveralls

3.2 Lockout/Tagout (LOTO):
- LOTO must be applied before any maintenance activity
- All energy sources must be isolated: electrical, hydraulic, pressure
- Permit-to-Work (PTW) required for all major maintenance

3.3 Isolation Procedure:
Step 1: Obtain PTW from area supervisor
Step 2: Stop pump at control panel
Step 3: Close isolation valves (suction and discharge)
Step 4: Apply LOTO on motor control panel
Step 5: Drain pump casing via drain plug
Step 6: Verify zero pressure with pressure gauge

4. INSPECTION CRITERIA AND THRESHOLDS
---------------------------------------
4.1 Vibration Thresholds:
- Normal: below 2.8 mm/s RMS
- Alert: 2.8 to 4.5 mm/s RMS (schedule inspection within 5 days)
- Critical: above 4.5 mm/s RMS (STOP pump immediately)

4.2 Bearing Temperature Thresholds:
- Normal operating temperature: 50-70°C
- Alert level: 70-85°C (monitor closely, check lubrication)
- Critical level: above 85°C (stop pump, replace bearings)
- Ambient temperature correction: measured temp minus ambient should not exceed 50°C

4.3 Mechanical Seal Condition:
- Acceptable leakage: less than 1 drop per minute (standard seals)
- Action leakage: 3 or more drops per minute (schedule replacement within 7 days)
- Inspect for: leakage, heat marks, face damage, spring condition

4.4 Performance Parameters:
- Flow rate deviation: Alert if >10% from design flow
- Head deviation: Alert if >8% from rated head
- Efficiency threshold: Replace impeller if efficiency drops below 65%

5. MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
------------------------
5.1 Daily Checks (Operator):
- Check pump for unusual noise or vibration
- Verify bearing temperature within limits
- Check mechanical seal for leaks
- Verify suction and discharge pressures
- Check motor current (should be within 10% of nameplate)

5.2 Weekly Maintenance:
- Grease bearings (if grease-lubricated type)
- Check alignment visually
- Verify coupling guard in place
- Clean pump exterior and drain pit

5.3 Monthly Maintenance:
- Vibration reading with portable analyzer
- Oil level check (if oil-lubricated type)
- Thermal imaging of motor and bearings
- Review operating log for trends

5.4 Annual Overhaul:
- Full disassembly and inspection
- Replace mechanical seals
- Replace bearings
- Check impeller clearances and wear
- Motor winding resistance test
- Realignment with laser alignment tool

6. PUMP REASSEMBLY PROCEDURE
------------------------------
Step 1: Clean all components with lint-free cloth
Step 2: Inspect wear ring clearances (replace if >2x original tolerance)
Step 3: Install new bearings (heat to 80°C in oil bath, do not exceed 100°C)
Step 4: Install new mechanical seal per manufacturer instructions
Step 5: Install impeller with correct torque (refer to manufacturer specs)
Step 6: Set impeller clearance (typically 0.3-0.5 mm, refer to datasheet)
Step 7: Reassemble casing with new gaskets
Step 8: Connect coupling (check gap and alignment)
Step 9: Remove LOTO, open isolation valves slowly
Step 10: Prime pump before starting
Step 11: Start pump and monitor for first 15 minutes
Step 12: Run-in period: 2 hours at 50% load, monitor temperature and vibration

7. TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
--------------------------
Problem: Excessive vibration
Causes: Imbalance, cavitation, misalignment, worn bearings, resonance
Action: Check alignment, check NPSH available, balance impeller, replace bearings

Problem: High bearing temperature
Causes: Over-greasing, under-greasing, bearing failure, misalignment, excessive load
Action: Check lubrication type and quantity, verify alignment, replace bearings if damaged

Problem: Mechanical seal leakage
Causes: Seal face damage, elastomer failure, incorrect installation, excessive pressure
Action: Inspect seal faces, replace elastomers, verify seal flush piping pressure

Problem: Low flow rate
Causes: Worn impeller, air entrainment, blocked suction strainer, valve partially closed
Action: Check strainer, verify valve positions, inspect impeller for wear or damage

Problem: Pump will not start
Causes: Motor overload trip, LOTO still applied, coupling failure
Action: Check MCC for trip, verify LOTO removed, inspect coupling
